Fibre Cement Duroboard

"RHINO" FIBRE CEMENT DUROBOARDS are made from a fine blend of imported mineral fibre and portland cement in our most modern, fully automated plant in Guwahati. They strictly conform to Indian Standard Specification IS-2098.1964.
The scientific blending of fibre and portland cement make "RHINO" DUROBOARD flexible, light in weight and at the same time strong. They are best suited for putting up false ceilings, partitions and panellings.
