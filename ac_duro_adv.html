Product Advantages
 
FIRE RETARDANT
 
Being non-combustible, they act as a barrier to and prevent the spread of fire, thus offering protection against fire hazards. Moreover, they are the best insulation against electricity.
 
WATER RESISTANT
 
The higher density and smooth surface of "RHINO" DUROBOARDS make them impervious to water and, where, they are used, they protect the interior from water or moisture damage.
 
ROT AND TERMITE PROOF
 
They are clean and smooth and do not harbour vermins, termites and rodents because pests cannot subsist on them; therefore, they need no preservative treatment.
 
DIMENSIONAL STABILITY
 
Being resistant to variations in temperature and humidity, they do not shrink, swell or crack and are thus best suited for a tropical country like India.
 
ECONOMICAL
 
They are economical as they do not require any maintenance because of their unique pest and weather resistant qualities. The smooth surface does not collect dust and they are easy to clean. Just a rub of damp cloth over the surface restores the original appearance.
