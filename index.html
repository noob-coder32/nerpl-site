About us

Fibre cement, engineered for tropical weather

RHINO products blend mineral fibre with portland cement, giving the flexural strength that steel gives to R.C.C. Sheets are made by vacuum corrugation at our modern automatic plant in Guwahati.

🛡️

Rigid & durable

Inert inorganic fibre means sheets resist weather and actually gain strength with age.

🔥

Non-combustible

Strength is unaffected at 100 °C, acting as a barrier to the spread of fire.

💧

Leak proof

High density makes the sheets impermeable, even through torrential rain.

🌪️

Wind resistant

Withstands high-velocity winds and cyclones when laid per the Code of Practice.

⚗️

Non-corrosive

Unaffected by chemical fumes, acids and gases. Vermin and rodents cannot damage it.

🔇

Acoustic comfort

Dampens sound and vibration for homes, offices and school sheds.
